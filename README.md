## 🎬 What is Ozil AI?

In 2016, Mesut Özil chipped the keeper. Then he rolled the ball into an empty net. In October 2026, the AI version of that goal went viral. It hit 3.8 million views on TikTok.

**Ozil AI puts you in that exact shot.** Upload one photo. Get a 15 second video of you scoring it. Same camera. Same keeper. Same sliding defenders.

## 📸 How it works

1. **Upload one photo.** One person. Face visible. Standing works best.
2. **AI renders the video.** Your whole self goes on the ball. Not just a face swap.
3. **Download and post.** Share it on TikTok, Reels or Shorts. Add the trending sound.

Rendering takes about 4 minutes. You can close the page. We email you when it is ready.

## ✨ Why Ozil AI

- 🎥 **The original 15 second shot.** Not a short clip in a fake stadium.
- 🧍 **Your whole self in the video.** Your clothes and your build stay.
- 📱 **Ready to post.** 9:16 vertical. 720p. No watermark.
- 🛡️ **Fair billing.** Failed renders are refunded automatically.
- ♾️ **Credits never expire.** One time packs only. No subscription.

## 🖼️ Photo tips

**Works best**

- One person in the photo
- Face clearly visible
- Even light
- Full body or waist up

**Avoid**

- Celebrity or cartoon images
- Group photos
- Sunglasses or masks
- Tiny photos under 300 px
